# Research publications

[PL](publications.md) · [EN](en-publications.md)

These manuscripts are at the conceptual development stage. The descriptions outline their intended research scope.

## AIM-ER model of emotion regulation

A theoretical paper on emotion regulation through AI-assisted music creation, aiming to synthesise the literature critically and formulate empirically testable hypotheses.

## From personal experience to music

A qualitative study of creators who use music generators to give musical form to personal experiences.

## Motives and functions of music creation

A survey examining motives for making music and its functions in emotion regulation.

## Alexithymia and emotional expression

An analysis of associations between alexithymia, emotional expression and emotional clarity in music creation.

## Measuring regulation functions

Development of the AI-MRFS scale informed by qualitative research, followed by evaluation of its psychometric properties.

## Rumination and avoidance

A study of associations between music creation, rumination and avoidance of emotional experiences.

## Emotional changes in everyday life

A diary study examining within-person changes in affect associated with individual creative sessions.

## Agency and perceived authorship

A planned experiment on the effects of creative control on agency and psychological ownership of the resulting music.

## Music and narrative identity

A comparison of ways of representing autobiographical experiences and their relationship to personal life narratives.
