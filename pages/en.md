# About the project

[PL](index.md) · [EN](en.md)

Music creation offers a way to express oneself, explore experiences and develop personal meaning. Generative artificial intelligence extends opportunities to engage in this activity, including for people without training in composition. The project takes its starting point from creators' accounts of making music in the context of their emotional experiences.

We examine how intention, choice of words, artistic decisions and revisiting one's own work relate to emotional clarity, agency and understanding personal experiences. We also consider situations in which creation does not provide anticipated relief, sustains rumination or interferes with everyday functioning.

AIM-ER is a research project of the Digital Psychology Student Research Group at VIZJA University. We are developing a theoretical model and study protocols. No empirical project findings are available yet.
