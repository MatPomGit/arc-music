# The psychology of AI-assisted music creation

[PL](index.md) · [EN](en.md)

ARC-MUSIC grew out of accounts from people who describe making music with generative artificial intelligence as a way of expressing difficult emotions and making sense of personal experiences.

Our research examines the psychological functions of this form of creativity, particularly emotion regulation, creative agency and the meaning creators attribute to their own works. We are interested in both the creative process and its place in participants' lives.

ARC-MUSIC is a project of the Digital Psychology Student Research Group within the Centre for Digital Psychology at VIZJA University.
