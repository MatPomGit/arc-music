# About the project

[PL](index.md) · [EN](en.md)

The project grew out of accounts from people who describe making music with generative artificial intelligence as a way of expressing difficult emotions and making sense of personal experiences

Our research examines the psychological functions of this form of creativity, particularly emotion regulation, creative agency and the meaning creators attribute to their own works. We are interested in both the creative process and its place in participants' lives

Project of the Digital Psychology Student Research Group at VIZJA University
