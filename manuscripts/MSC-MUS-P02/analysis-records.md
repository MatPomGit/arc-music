# Blank qualitative analysis records

These are field specifications, not participant data or a mandatory codebook. Completed records, recordings, transcripts, extracts and case-level summaries must stay in approved institutional storage. Do not commit populated copies to Git.

| Record | Fields |
|---|---|
| Interview log | Pseudonymous ID; guide version; recording consent verified; date in protected log; duration; record type (transcript or approved notes); latest eligible session discussed (yes/no/not elicited); optional consented selection category (privacy/recall/other/not stated); optional contrast; deviations; unrecorded artefact showing (yes/no/not elicited); no transfer by default; recording/storage status; withdrawal status. |
| Episode memo | Episode ID; optional consented selection category (no reason required); recency and uncertainty; context; reported intent before session; changes in goal; operations in reported order; remembered experience versus current interpretation; intervening events; turning points; perceived contribution and ownership if raised; personal meaning; immediate and later experience; other explanations; missing context; analyst reflection. |
| Reflexivity memo | Researcher role; prior assumptions; interview interaction; leading probes or changes in direction; alternative interpretations; analytic decisions; next review. |
| Candidate theme memo | Working name; central organising concept; interpretive claim; supporting extract references; divergent cases; boundaries; relationship to other themes; change history; theoretical implications and limits. |
| Sampling review | Completed interviews; diversity of relevant contexts; depth and contrasting accounts; gaps; feasibility; reasons for continuing or stopping; protocol version. |
| Translation review | Protected source extract reference; English rendering; ambiguous terms; reviewer rationale; de-identification decision; quotation/paraphrase status. |
| Pilot review | Guide version; understanding; neutral prompts; contextual depth; duration; consent and recording; keep/revise/suspend; justification; pilot corpus eligibility and approval. |
| Construct transfer | Theme and interpretation; relevant contrasts; proposed construct or design implication; reflective/formative/contextual status if justified; candidate indicator; retain/revise/defer; rationale; validation needs. |
| Deviation log | Protocol version; departure; context; impact on interpretation or participant safety; decision and approval where applicable. |

Do not force all fields to be filled; mark not elicited, not applicable or uncertain separately. Records support reflexive analysis, not inter-rater agreement or prevalence estimates. Preserve a clear distinction between reported experience, interpretation and proposed theory. A theme is a pattern of shared meaning organised around a central concept; an interview domain alone is not a theme.

Pre-collection gate: institutional ethics and storage approval, consent/withdrawal and distress procedures, approved channel permissions, trained interviewer, recording/transcription decision and guide readiness for approved pilot. Before main recruitment: documented pilot decision. Pre-reporting gate: actual analytic corpus, reflexivity and thematic account, de-identification review, RTARG reporting, translation decisions, actual deviations and limitations, author-confirmed declarations and independent scientific review.
