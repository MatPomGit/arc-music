Return itemised blocking/major/minor findings with source path and concrete correction; assess feasibility separately from scientific merit. A high score cannot waive ethics approval, actual data collection, final source audit or independent review. Do not require kappa, saturation counts, or member checking as proof of a uniquely correct interpretation. No results or findings may be invented. Final publication readiness requires actual analysis and reporting, not only protocol review.
