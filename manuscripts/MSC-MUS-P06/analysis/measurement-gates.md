# Measurement and evidence gates v0.8.0

| Construct | Proposed measure | Evidence and remaining gate |
|---|---|---|
| Trait rumination | Polish RRS-R | Kornacka et al. (2016), alpha .74 brooding, .77 reflection, n=99; confirm exact version, scoring and permission |
| Trait experiential avoidance | Polish BEAQ | Wardęszkiewicz & Holas (2024), N=1216, two-factor solution; alpha .78 cognitive-emotional, .74 behavioural; 21-day test-retest r=.79/.75; verify authorized scoring and rights |
| Music engagement | HUMS concept only | Adolescent/young samples, not validated for adult AI creation |
| Session avoidance | Original anchored 0–10 rating | Cognitive interviews, within-person variation, validity evidence |
| Interference | Original 0–10 rating tied to prior planned action | Cognitive interviews; compare with completion self-report |
| Brooding/reflection | Separate proposed state indicators | Pilot interpretation; no diagnostic cutoffs |

Polish RRS-R and BEAQ are candidate baseline descriptors, not required primary covariates or validated session-level instruments. Do not copy items until rights and exact versions are established. The BEAQ validation article is CC BY 4.0; this does not establish unrestricted rights to the underlying instrument. No questionnaire items are copied here. No clinical classification or intervention.

Use subscale scores only after verification of item mapping, permissible missingness and scoring. Do not compute a total BEAQ score by default. Assess sample-specific reliability with uncertainty, and distinguish between-person and within-person variation. A single diary item has no internal consistency coefficient. See pilot-measurement.md and bibliography-audit.md.
