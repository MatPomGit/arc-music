# Measurement and evidence gates

| Construct | Proposed measure | Gate |
|---|---|---|
| Trait rumination | RRS brooding/reflection distinction | Verify adult Polish version, access, scoring and rights |
| Trait experiential avoidance | BEAQ candidate | Verify Polish adaptation and use conditions |
| Music engagement | HUMS as conceptual reference | Do not transplant adolescent listening score to AI creators |
| Session avoidance | New anchored 0–10 rating | Cognitive interviews, variance, validity evidence |
| Interference | New 0–10 rating tied to prior planned action | Cognitive interviews; compare with completion self-report |
| Brooding/reflection | Separate proposed state indicators | Pilot interpretation; no diagnostic cutoffs |

DOIs in references.bib are provisional and require verification against bibliographic records. This is a targeted foundational bibliography, not a systematic search or full-text audit. Direct AI creation evidence remains a gap. Record the version, language, permissions, source and scoring protocol for each selected instrument before use; no questionnaire text is copied. Do not infer mental disorder or harmful engagement solely from negative lyrics, repeated generation, or a score.
