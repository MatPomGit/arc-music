# P06 construct-to-item map (candidate v1.0.0)

Scientific owner: Mateusz Pomianek. All diary items are original proposed indicators, not validated scales. Legacy column names are preserved for historical reproducibility. No new empirical observations.

| Construct | Field | Time and anchors | Estimand / role | Interpretation limit |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Session-specific avoidance motive | avoidance | 0–15 min before start; 0 not at all, 10 very strongly | H1 within-person slope per point, H2 interaction | Not trait experiential avoidance, not a diagnosis |
| Current distress | distress_pre, distress_post | before and 0–15 min after; 0 none, 10 very strong | Baseline covariate; difference pre minus post is relief | Not functioning or causal change |
| Expected activity difficulty | expected_difficulty | pre; 0 not difficult, 10 extremely difficult | Pre-session covariate | Not realized obstacles |
| Brooding-like experience | brooding | post; unwanted recurrent thoughts without progress, 0–10 | H3 within-person association adjusted for relief and pre variables | Not RRS trait score; retrospective session rating |
| Perceived insight | reflection (legacy) | post; new perspective on own experience, 0–10 | Exploratory only | Not reflective pondering or an objective insight test |
| Interference with specified valued activity | interference | 24–28 h after end; fixed first 24 h; 0 no obstacles, 10 prevented performance | H1–H3 observed defined endpoint; full-session target requires applicability | Includes contextual obstacles, not attributed to music |
| Activity completion | activity_completed | same matched activity, first 24 h; yes/no/skip | Separate descriptive behavioral self-report | No automatic mapping to interference |
| Applicability | activity_relevant, activity_status_reason | matched timely follow-up; still relevant, prevented by obstacles, independently inapplicable, unknown | Defines endpoint availability gate; do not code zero or impute undefined | Not a postexposure causal adjustment |
| Delayed distress | distress_followup | 24–28 h; 0–10 | Exploratory only | Not interference or P01 H6 confirmation |
| Creative operations | generation_count, duration_minutes, technical_failure | post; count, elapsed minutes, yes/no/skip | Descriptive/exploratory | Iteration is not rumination |

Baseline RRS-R and BEAQ are candidate trait measures with separate authorization/scoring and no substitution for state items. Pilot checks: distinguish deliberate distraction vs involuntary repetitive thought, a new perspective vs a reflection process, relief vs ability to act, a prevented activity vs independent inapplicability, and a barrier from the cause of the barrier. Two rounds, record misunderstandings and revisions; no significance-based item selection. The numerical confounding diagnostic assumes a single hypothetical within-person omitted variable, does not identify a causal effect, and does not change the primary model.
