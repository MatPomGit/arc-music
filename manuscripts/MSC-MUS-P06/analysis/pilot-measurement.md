# Measurement pilot plan (prospective)

Conduct two iterative rounds of cognitive interviews (provisional 5–8 adults per round) with different levels of AI music experience. Use think-aloud and comprehension probes in Polish, including examples of purposeful distraction, artistic iteration and unwanted repetitive thought. Check whether interference refers to the same intended activity, not the perceived effect of the song. Use a random activity identifier rather than private text by default. Activity category is not part of the current analytic export; any proposal to collect it requires explicit approval and a versioned schema change before use. Use anchored 0–10 ratings, separately ask completion yes/no; avoid wording implying blame or clinical harm.

Then test the revised diary in 15–25 volunteers for 14 days. Record planned and observed check-ins, timestamps, invalid responses, missingness and burden; evaluate distribution, proportion at 0/10, within-person SD, ICC with uncertainty where estimable, and agreement with completion status. Interpret single-item validity as preliminary evidence rather than internal consistency. Assess whether participants understand differences between relief, session avoidance motivation, brooding and perceived insight (not a reflection-process measure), and whether avoiding a thought can be deliberate and functional.

Provisional gates: median at least four eligible sessions per enrolled participant (including zero), at least 70% eligible sessions with timely matched applicable follow-up and observed interference, no extreme floor/ceiling concentration (review if >50% of observed ratings at one endpoint), and no repeated major comprehension issue after revision. Use all eligible logged sessions as the follow-up denominator; report defined, undefined and unknown applicability separately, and do not treat a zero denominator as a pass. These candidate definitions require approval before use. These thresholds guide feasibility, not significance selection. Document all failures and revisions. Do not use the pilot to select a favorable hypothesis or estimate a precise smallest effect. If inadequate variation or poor burden, revise the design before locking the confirmatory protocol.

For licensed multi-item baseline instruments, verify exact language/version, scoring, missing-item rules and rights; report subscale reliability estimates with intervals only with sufficient data. Do not estimate complex CFA in the small pilot. Retain the BEAQ two-dimensional structure for planned descriptive use unless an independently justified alternative is preregistered. Trait ratings are not state measures. Recruitment and testing require ethics approval; no participants have been recruited.

Protocol v0.10.0: use unique activity tokens; pilot verification must distinguish a mismatched/unknown activity, an independently inapplicable endpoint and a defined activity prevented by obstacles. Test whether participants can distinguish barriers from the cause of barriers; never code missing or undefined interference as zero. See construct-map.md. Primary follow-up 24–28 h from recorded end; test 24–26 h sensitivity and recall of the fixed first 24 h. Report these reasons separately from missed check-ins. Document whether changes to planned activities are systematic by pre-session avoidance. Do not select a window by significance.

## Supplementary pilot candidates

Following the existing diary outcomes, ask STOP_DIFF only when the person had an intention to stop: „Na ile trudno było zakończyć sesję wtedy, kiedy chciałeś ją zakończyć?” (0–10). Ask whether repeated attempts involved creative refinement, repeated focus on the same concern, both, other, or no repetition. At next-day follow-up, after interference, ask whether another regulation opportunity occurred; record SWITCH only when one occurred. Distinguish no opportunity, undefined, refusal and nonresponse. Document comprehension, added duration and within-person variation. The current schema, primary FE contrast and secondary family are unchanged; these candidates need separate approval and an export revision.
