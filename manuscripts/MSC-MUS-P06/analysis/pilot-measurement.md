# Measurement pilot plan (prospective)

Conduct two iterative rounds of cognitive interviews (provisional 5–8 adults per round) with different levels of AI music experience. Use think-aloud and comprehension probes in Polish, including examples of purposeful distraction, artistic iteration and unwanted repetitive thought. Check whether interference refers to the same intended activity, not the perceived effect of the song. Record activity category and a random identifier rather than private text by default. Use anchored 0–10 ratings, separately ask completion yes/no; avoid wording implying blame or clinical harm.

Then test the revised diary in 15–25 volunteers for 14 days. Record planned and observed check-ins, timestamps, invalid responses, missingness and burden; evaluate distribution, proportion at 0/10, within-person SD, ICC with uncertainty where estimable, and agreement with completion status. Interpret single-item validity as preliminary evidence rather than internal consistency. Assess whether participants understand differences between relief, avoidance, brooding and reflection, and whether avoiding a thought can be deliberate and functional.

Provisional gates: median at least four eligible sessions, at least 70% valid follow-ups, no extreme floor/ceiling concentration (review if >50% at one endpoint), and no repeated major comprehension issue after revision. These thresholds guide feasibility, not significance selection. Document all failures and revisions. Do not use the pilot to select a favorable hypothesis or estimate a precise smallest effect. If inadequate variation or poor burden, revise the design before locking the confirmatory protocol.

For licensed multi-item baseline instruments, verify exact language/version, scoring, missing-item rules and rights; report subscale reliability estimates with intervals only with sufficient data. Do not estimate complex CFA in the small pilot. Retain the BEAQ two-dimensional structure for planned descriptive use unless an independently justified alternative is preregistered. Trait ratings are not state measures. Recruitment and testing require ethics approval; no participants have been recruited.
