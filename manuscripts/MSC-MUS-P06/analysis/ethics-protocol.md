# P06: prospective ethics and data handling protocol

Status: draft for institutional approval, not approval or a live recruitment document.

## Recruitment and voluntary participation
Recruit adults who already create music with AI. Do not recruit on the basis of distress disclosures or scrape community messages. Obtain permission for recruitment channels and use a neutral study description. Explain that participation is observational, not treatment or crisis monitoring. Do not require an AI account purchase, extra sessions, distress induction, lyrics, diagnosis or upload of songs. Consent must precede any research data collection. Participants may skip any rating or discontinue without penalty. No clinical threshold or automated individual feedback is inferred from proposed ratings.

## Participant safety
If a session becomes uncomfortable, participants may stop the diary or music creation and seek support of their choosing. Provide institutionally reviewed local resources and an emergency contact route appropriate to the participant country; in Poland, 112 for immediate danger. Do not promise monitoring or emergency intervention from an asynchronous questionnaire. If a participant directly contacts the research team about serious distress, use a separately approved response procedure specifying responsible staff, response times and limits of confidentiality. Do not ask for details of traumatic events. Adverse experiences, burden and optional reasons for stopping are reported in aggregate; no extra sensitive free-text fields by default.

## Data minimization and security
Assign opaque participant and activity identifiers. Keep consent and reidentification keys separate from analytic records in institution-authorized storage. Do not retain prompts, song audio, private activity text, credentials or diagnoses by default. Local activity label handling, access controls, retention, withdrawal, deletion, backups and any data processor must be approved by the university before a field application is deployed. A random token verifies linkage, not the content or truth of the action. Do not collect participant data in Git, CI, public website, synthetic fixtures or external AI services. Export only approved variables; perform a disclosure-risk review before releasing aggregated results. This protocol follows the repository data policy.

## Approval checklist
Before any interviews or pilot: ethics review and documented authorization; approved participant information and consent; data controller and lawful basis confirmed with university; storage/access/retention and withdrawal procedure; language and rights for each optional standardized instrument; approved recruitment and distress response. Before confirmatory recruitment: separate pilot results, revised and frozen diary, preregistration, final sample design, reproducible analysis and review. All are pending unless separately documented. Neither code completion nor a green CI substitutes for approval.
