# Paired missing-outcome calibration v0.7.0

Executed locally in Python with NumPy/SciPy and one BLAS thread, seed 2608, n=160, m=6, 12 cells x 200 replications = 2400 successful paired synthetic datasets, zero failures. Reproduce with OPENBLAS_NUM_THREADS=1 OMP_NUM_THREADS=1 python missing_calibration.py. Rounded results are preserved in missing-calibration-results.csv. The separate NumPy implementation mirrors weighted fixed effects and deterministic shifts; equivalence checks are provided, and exact-head CI remains pending.

The target is the realized full-data within-person coefficient from the same simulated dataset, not the latent slope or an exact population parameter. MNAR adds outcome dependence and an avoidance-by-outcome interaction to the missingness logit. All predictor values remain available; this is not a simulation of unlogged sessions or dropout.

At latent slope .15 and 30% expected missingness, MAR paired bias is -.00171 for complete cases and -.00227 for IPW (cap 10); corresponding RMSE .02309 and .02431. For MNAR, bias is -.12865 and -.13996, RMSE .13125 and .14268. Under 45% MNAR missingness, paired bias is -.16614 and -.17778 respectively. In these scenarios the estimated marginal IPW model does not resolve outcome-dependent selection and can worsen bias. With latent slope zero and 45% MNAR, negative bias is -.15520 (complete cases). These are not empirical findings.

The 15 deterministic shift scenarios bracket the realized full-data coefficient in 98.5–100% of simulated datasets, but the mean width is .308–.551 slope points. The envelope is neither a 95% confidence interval nor a calibrated probability bound; it is a broad sensitivity grid, with arbitrary shift magnitudes not empirically justified. Bootstrap interval coverage has not been calibrated. Do not select shifts according to significance.

Keep complete cases as the provisional main specification and IPW as a sensitivity analysis, not a corrective guarantee. Report all shift scenarios and distinguish changes in direction from shifts across a substantively justified threshold. Inability to rule out plausible MNAR alternatives should limit conclusions. Next: calibrate bootstrap intervals and pairwise inclusion weighting; model unlogged sessions separately; justify shift grid with pilot information. 200 replications per cell constitute preliminary screening.
