# Numerical evidence gate v0.12.0

The historical CSV tables and narrative numbers from v0.5.0–v0.7.0 do not yet have independently reproduced, exact-HEAD full-run artifacts. Passing the regular CI smoke grid does not verify them. Treat all historical numerical values as provisional, not validated evidence for sample size or inferential calibration. The reproduction matrix executes the original default seeds and replication counts, records actual outputs and library versions, compares against the archived CSV at its displayed precision, and fails on discrepancies. A mismatch must be investigated, not suppressed or resolved by silently editing expected numbers. Preserve both historical and actual results until reconciled.

Bootstrap calibration calls the production participant bootstrap with complete refitting. The target is a separate large complete-data population-reference coefficient (exact zero for the symmetric null), not the realized full-data sample slope. It evaluates IPW cap-10 percentile-interval coverage, bias, widths and valid draws; the shift envelope remains a scenario range and not a confidence interval. Under MNAR the complete-data target need not be identified by IPW. The short CI calibration is screening only. At least 1000 outer replicates and 499 inner draws are planned per retained cell, with computational feasibility and MC uncertainty reported; no full bootstrap result is claimed here.

Regular CI and reproduction CI are separate. A failed reproduction gate blocks numerical claims and final release. No independent review, participant data or ethics approval is implied.
