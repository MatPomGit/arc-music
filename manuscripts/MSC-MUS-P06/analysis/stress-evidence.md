# Dropout and heterogeneity stress test v0.6.0

Executed locally using Python, NumPy 2.3.5 and SciPy 1.17.0, single BLAS thread. Seed 2607, 36 cells x 300 replicates = 10800 successful datasets, zero failures. Six independent reference datasets of 30000 participants. Reproduce with OPENBLAS_NUM_THREADS=1 OMP_NUM_THREADS=1 python stress.py. Rounded outputs are in stress-results.csv; the script emits full precision, coverage and Monte Carlo uncertainty. Exact-head CI reproduction is pending.

n=160, latent slope 0/.15; monotone attrition calibrated to 15/30/45% by the last planned session, not the percentage of lost observations. Heterogeneous residual SD (lognormal dispersion .30), random slope SD .35. Four/six/ten planned sessions paired with rho .1/.4/.7 respectively. These factors are jointly varied, not separately identified. MAR-style risk depends on the simulated current distress predictor; MNAR-style risk additionally depends on current outcome. In real dropout the current distress may itself be unobserved, so MAR is conditional on covariate availability. This is a DGP label, not a guarantee of ignorability or a correction for unlogged sessions.

For .15 latent slope and MAR-style attrition, full CR1 rejection rates at 30% dropout are .627/.847/.963 for four/six/ten sessions. At 45% they are .557/.777/.950. Nested correction rates at 30% are .733/.883/.963. Under the null MAR-style dropout, full correction ranges .020–.057 and nested .030–.063. The maximum MNAR-style null rejection for nested is .077. At 300 repetitions, Monte Carlo SE near .05 is .0126; this is stress screening, not final calibration. The full correction remains the provisional primary method.

Design implication: 160 enrollees is not sufficient under all plausible session/attrition patterns. Six sessions may be inadequate at 45% dropout; ten planned sessions are more robust in this slice but feasibility and participant burden require pilot evidence. Do not prescribe music generation to achieve a quota in an observational study. Expand n and independently vary rho, heterogeneity, selection, and session frequency; at least 1000 replicates per retained design cell. Evaluate missingness correction when predictors remain available and separately when whole sessions are absent. Prior 27000-replication evidence is not independently reproduced by this new stress test. No empirical participant data.
