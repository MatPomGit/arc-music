# Completion-dependent calibration
Status: PROSPECTIVE; all records generated synthetically. No new empirical observations.
The six scenarios use N=1000, true raw composite interactions 0, .54, .72, and two response mechanisms. Each scenario requests 120 independent datasets, 50 imputations per method, a fixed seed and paired observation regimes. The conditional composite mean is linear with the target coefficient; the probability of a meaningful sketch depends on arm, trait and their interaction, and conditional fit has mean equal to composite mean divided by completion probability. This deliberately isolates calibration of the composite estimand, not a mechanistic model of distinct completion and fit effects. Baseline covariates correlate with the moderator, and bounded integer fit has arm-specific heteroskedasticity.

A rating response depends on baseline and true completion status in both mechanisms, with additional dependence on unobserved fit in the outcome-dependent mechanism. Neither is necessarily MAR for the composite conditional on baseline alone. Status capture among nonresponders is 45%/25% for meaningful/absent sketches in the basic regime and 90%/85% in the hypothetical enhanced regime. The same uniforms and rating responses are used across regimes; enhanced status capture cannot erase known outcomes. These are hypothetical probabilities, not measurements or feasibility promises. Known absence defines zero even without a fit rating; a known meaningful sketch with missing rating remains missing. Unknown status never defines zero.

Compare (1) baseline-only PMM with basic capture, (2) status-informed PMM with basic capture, and (3) status-informed PMM with enhanced capture. For known meaningful sketches, the supplementary estimator imputes applicable missing fit from confirmed meaningful donors within each arm; for unknown status it uses baseline-only composite PMM. This simple hybrid is not a joint model for status and fit, and may remain biased when status itself is selectively missing. The primary estimator is unchanged. All outcome regressions use the original baseline design, never conditioning on postassignment completion. The grid shifts imputed values only. Report nominal rejection, coverage, bias, RMSE, grid-robust rejection and Wilson Monte Carlo intervals, missing fractions and paired differences with Monte Carlo SE. The 120-replication run is targeted calibration with limited precision, not definitive power evidence. Failures are retained and fail CI rather than silently dropping datasets.

Run: OPENBLAS_NUM_THREADS=1 OMP_NUM_THREADS=1 python completion_calibration.py --reps 120 --imputations 50. The dedicated workflow records the exact source SHA and JSON results. No results asserted until the artefact is inspected.
