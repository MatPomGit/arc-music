# Measurement evidence
- Full Polish PAQ: Larionow, Preece and Mudło-Głagolska (2022), https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyt.2022.1047191. Full text https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9748568/ (Table 2): N=1008; N-DDF omega .90, CI [.89,.91]; N-DIF omega .89, CI [.88,.90]. This supports facet assessment, not the task-specific endpoint or invariance in the new experiment.
- Original PAQ: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.paid.2018.05.011. Full 24-item measure distinguishes appraisal of positive and negative feelings. Short form is not a substitute for facet-level moderation.
- Polish instrument and scoring instructions: https://repozytorium.ukw.edu.pl/handle/item/8068. The developers' questionnaire page https://psychologywa.com/questionnaires/ states their measures are available for use without charge. The Polish edition's online administration and redistribution terms must be recorded before recruitment; no item text is copied here.
- S-DERS: https://doi.org/10.1177/1073191115601218, https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5243864/. Initial evidence in 484 young adult women; a validated Polish state version was not established in the targeted search. It remains optional, not part of confirmatory outcomes. Polish trait DERS-8 validation does not establish state sensitivity.
- Lane and Smith (2021), https://doi.org/10.3390/jintelligence9030042, distinguishes emotional awareness as a skill from self-report; this study does not implement a performance-based measure.
- Huang et al. (2020), https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.4245530, reports creative workflow evidence, not an emotional intervention.
Checked 2026-11-26. No study-specific permissions, ethics approval or validation of the task-specific ratings are claimed. Further full-text claim audit and stimulus rights checks are needed.
