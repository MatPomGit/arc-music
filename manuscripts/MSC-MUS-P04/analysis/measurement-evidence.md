# Current instrument: PAQ-S-PL
Decision 2026-11-26: research team reports access to version S. The full PAQ evidence and prior scoring implementation are historical, not the current protocol. The Polish short-form scoring instructions https://repozytorium.ukw.edu.pl/handle/item/8072 explicitly specify a single overall score, six items scored 1–7 summed to 6–42; no component scores. Original short form https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jad.2023.01.036. Polish validation https://doi.org/10.5603/jsmh.97351 (2023 journal volume 21, pages 54–63, online publication February 2024), https://repozytorium.ukw.edu.pl/handle/item/8143. Publisher full-text Tables 1–2: N=1115, ages 18–72, mean 19.78, SD 8.91, omega and alpha .81 (95% CI .79–.82), one-factor CFI .983, TLI .958, RMSEA .077, SRMR .020. The observed total is the moderator; no item proxies or separate facet scores. Complete-case scoring before randomisation is a protocol choice. No permission to reproduce item wording in the study interface is inferred from access. Sample-specific reliability and validity remain untested. Original full PAQ validation (https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyt.2022.1047191) provides construct background only.
S-DERS remains optional: https://doi.org/10.1177/1073191115601218; no established Polish state adaptation in the prior targeted check. The neuropsychological literature is described in main.tex; perception, interoception and ERP findings do not establish an AI intervention mechanism. Earlier detailed claim audit is available in Git history. Full-text audit of all citations and stimulus rights remain required.
