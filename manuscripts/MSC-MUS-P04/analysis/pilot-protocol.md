# P04 pilot and preregistration readiness
Status: PROSPECTIVE. No participant recruitment or approval is claimed. This is a proposed operational procedure; thresholds and materials must be agreed before piloting.

## Task specification
Both arms use identical rights-cleared instrumental excerpts, duration, player, ordering procedure, editor, eight-minute budget and instructions. Pre-generate and freeze the suggestion bank; include diverse negative and mixed-affect descriptions, neutral wording and reject-all. Avoid diagnostic labels, trauma prompts, personalised medical advice and steering to a particular emotion. Control arm receives identical editing functions and neutral interface guidance, but no emotion-language suggestions. The availability contrast includes semantic content and choice opportunities; it cannot isolate AI authorship. Participants may use a fictional mild event and do not have to reveal their narrative. Capture only numeric ratings and coarse operations; personal text remains local and is discarded.

Before any recruitment, complete and freeze [stimulus-manifest.csv](stimulus-manifest.csv), the canonical register of approved instrumental excerpts and the Polish emotion-language bank. The header-only register records no materials or clearance and does not permit recruitment. Use one row per instrumental excerpt (`instrumental_excerpt`) and one per frozen bank (`emotion_language_bank`), with unique asset IDs, source, model and version where applicable, ISO 8601 generation and editing dates, version, rights basis and reference to publicly shareable clearance evidence, and SHA-256 of the frozen asset. Record duration in seconds and the loudness-normalisation procedure for instrumental excerpts; leave these fields empty for the language bank. Use the bank's version field for its Polish wording version. Do not enter private links, personal information or confidential rights documents. Unverified rights or missing required metadata block material freeze. Do not upload unlicensed stimuli or confidential material. The actual stimulus set and software are not implemented by this protocol.

## Independent stimulus assessment before freeze

Use at least three independent assessors proficient in Polish, including expertise in emotion psychology and music, with declared relationships to the team. This is a proposed content review, not psychometric validation. Review all candidate phrases and excerpts without information about hypotheses or target participant PAQ-S scores. For each phrase, rate linguistic comprehensibility, affective specificity, contextual neutrality and freedom from diagnostic or directive content (1 = unacceptable, 4 = fully acceptable); provide a nonpersonal rationale for any rating of 1 or 2. For each excerpt, verify audio quality, absence of intelligible lyrics, common duration, loudness procedure and usability. Record perceived emotional character descriptively; do not require a single correct interpretation or select stimuli by their effect on the primary outcome. Resolve disagreement by revision and a new independent rating of revised materials. As a proposed conservative release rule, require every assessor to rate all four phrase criteria at least 3 and no unresolved safety, technical or rights concern. Retain the number of reviewed, revised and rejected candidates and item-level rating distributions in authorised institutional storage. Do not select or exclude material using confirmatory outcomes.

Run `python stimulus_manifest.py` from this directory after populating the register. A nonzero exit blocks material freeze. The script checks structure, dates (YYYY-MM-DD), unique identifiers and hash syntax, metadata completeness, exactly one language bank and at least two distinct instrumental choices. The two-choice minimum is structural, not a final bank size. Record a public HTTPS clearance reference without query or fragment; model and model version are required together when applicable. An empty editing date denotes no editing. Passing checks does not verify copyright, remote evidence, actual file hashes, audio properties, assessor agreement or approval. Independently recompute SHA-256 on the final files held in authorised storage and reconcile with the register; record the exact version, review evidence and freeze decision before pilot. Reopen review after any change. The empty register must remain BLOCKED until materials exist.

## Proposed pilot
Conduct 8–12 cognitive interviews and an independent 30–50-person feasibility pilot, with ethics/consent and data-governance clearance. Use think-aloud or retrospective probing of instructions without retaining personal emotional content. Ask participants to paraphrase the difference between fit, clarity, and meaningful-sketch status; check whether both groups can reject options and whether the control interface is usable. Freeze wording after resolving recurrent misunderstandings. Do not include pilot participants in the confirmatory dataset or use the pilot interaction estimate to choose a target effect.

Proposed review triggers, not validated cutoffs: less than 80% of randomised participants with defined observed composite outcomes, less than 80% completing the interface without technical failure, recurrent confusion of fit with clarity, or more than 30% ratings at a single endpoint among meaningful sketches. Report counts and Wilson intervals by arm; small pilot estimates are imprecise. Any serious adverse event requires stopping and institutional review. Review all withdrawals and accessibility issues before launching. The zero composite is only for explicitly confirmed absence of a meaningful sketch; technical failure or withdrawal alone never defines zero.

## Smallest meaningful effect
Use structured elicitation before seeing confirmatory data: ask prospective users and independent researchers to judge interpretable differences in expected composite points between people at -1 and +1 SD PAQ-S total, supported by vignettes showing completion and fit separately. A difference-in-differences across those moderator levels is twice the linear interaction coefficient. Explain the composite tradeoff; a higher score can reflect greater completion or higher applicable fit. Record rationale, disagreement, target raw-unit interaction, feasibility and sensitivity assumptions. The .36/.54/.72-point scenarios are not established minimally important effects. Choose a fixed target only after this exercise and Monte Carlo uncertainty review. If the required sample is infeasible at zero additional cost, designate the study exploratory before data collection rather than lowering the evidential threshold.

## Required preregistration decisions
Confirm primary and secondary estimands, authorised PAQ-S total scoring, endpoint wording, final material manifest, randomisation implementation, missingness sensitivity grid, minimum meaningful effect and fixed recruitment cap/date, final analysis code and exact-head CI evidence. Record institutional approvals, consent and withdrawal procedure, data-retention policy and rights clearance. Obtain a scientific review by someone independent of protocol authorship. None of these approvals is implied by passing CI.


Before pilot freeze, test separate status questions for confirmed meaningful sketch, confirmed absence and unknown status, including technical interruption, explicit noncompletion, missing fit after confirmed production and withdrawal. No meaningful sketch is not automatically equivalent to a missing fit rating. Use a single approved ledger and the cohort validator in empirical.py; report production and conditional fit descriptively and the composite across assignment. A shift in composite moderation may arise from production alone. No component-specific causal conclusion is identified by conditioning on observed production. Verify electronic instrument permission separately; no recruitment until approved.

## Candidate supplementary pilot probes

After the existing unaided clarity outcome, assess EXP_LABEL (0 to 10: how well the words matched the emotion the participant intended to express). In the supported arm ask whether a suggestion was seen and used; record not applicable separately. Assess perceived musical fit only when an excerpt or sketch was experienced. These probes are not objective emotion-recognition tests; do not retain personal narrative. Record comprehension, timing, applicability and burden. Do not change the current export or PAQ-S moderator; an approved new version is required before exploratory inference.
