# Sample-size decision protocol v0.9.0

**Current method boundary (2026-11-28).** All new execution is Python-only. The one-factor, correlated-seven and higher-order development models use ordinal moment fitting and a moment-matched residual test, not WLSMV. Earlier external-reference computations and paired comparisons describe historical revisions, not a validated current estimator. Independent Python ordinal reference, robust fit/invariance equivalence, full calibration and scientific approval remain blocked. Archived evidence is retained; no results are transferred to the new estimator.

Status: prospective, not frozen. The existing N=400 EFA and N=500 CFA are planning values, not demonstrated requirements.

- Future candidate N: 260, 400, 500, 660, 800 in independent EFA and CFA samples. For group invariance use balanced and 70:30 allocation, and check minimum category counts separately.
- Core CFA grid: seven correlated factors, five items each, loading .60/.75, correlation .20/.50, balanced and moderately skewed categories. Stress: loading .40, correlation .80, cross-loadings, local dependence, and rare response categories. Include 0/10/20% item missingness with separately specified mechanisms. Do not equate 20% item missingness with 20% incomplete records.
- EFA: evaluate recovery of factor number via the prespecified 500-permutation procedure, assignment accuracy and loading error. Do not use knowledge of generating factor number for the recovery gate.
- CFA: proper convergence at least .95, absolute loading bias at most .05, marginal 95% coverage .92--.98 and type-I-error calibration; also report failures unconditionally. Assess validity hypotheses at meaningful observed-score associations fixed before data collection. Current latent comparator association .25 is a simulation input, not a clinically established effect.
- For each core scenario use at least 1000 replications, increasing to 2000 near a decision boundary. Report Wilson intervals and Monte Carlo standard errors; use independent seeds across scenarios and reproducible child seeds. An inconclusive interval requires more simulation, not automatic acceptance.
- Select the smallest candidate N meeting the frozen criteria across all core scenarios; report stress failures, group-specific requirements, and feasibility. If no N passes, revise the design, do not claim a power guarantee. Freeze recruitment caps before participant enrolment.
- The present validity calibration is a small development screen (30 replicates per scenario, 199 bootstrap resamples). It does not evaluate full EFA/CFA recovery, 5000-resample inference, or measurement-model uncertainty. The final decision remains blocked pending reference estimator validation and the larger calibrated grid.

Historical grids retain their actual N (including 250/650 where used); these rounded candidates require new calibration. The provisional EFA submission cap is 500, CFA cap 600; neither is approved.

## Conditional design decision after the diagnostic screen

Treat 400 EFA and 500 CFA records as separate provisional budget anchors. Proceed to sample-size calibration of the frozen model only if the pilot supports category use and the candidate domains have distinguishable content. The current screen found 4/12 proper fits at N=800 under joint weak loading and high factor correlation. Do not substitute 800 for a defensible minimum in this stress case. Revise empirically overlapping or weakly represented domains before simulating a new, explicitly versioned model. A simpler structure is a new hypothesis to confirm on an independent sample. The diagnostic work does not replace the candidate-N grid or justify enrolling any participants.

## Independent model comparison

The seven correlated functions remain a theoretical working hypothesis; a one-factor model tests the alternative of undifferentiated perceived regulatory engagement. A higher-order general factor is a separate provisional hypothesis about shared variance across functions, not a basis for a total score. These comparisons do not license merging domains. The reference ordinal WLSMV screen compares all three on the same independent CFA sample; EFA count recovery is evaluated on a different synthetic sample. Under equicorrelated generating factors, correlated and higher-order structures can be observationally close, so fit alone cannot identify the substantive model. Report proper fit rates across all requested draws, failures and reference fit measures; do not compare WLSMV p-values as if they were a regular nested likelihood-ratio test at correlation boundaries. Item MCAR complete-case stress tests are not a validated missingness strategy. Run model_comparison.py with --smoke for execution only; use 500 parallel-analysis permutations for the protocol and independent larger replication sets after the content model is frozen. Empirical content evidence, independent EFA/CFA and the existing prospective adequacy gates remain required.

## Distinct generating structures

The new alternative_model_calibration.py preserves all historical equicorrelated runs and separately generates a true one-factor model, a higher-order model with heterogeneous general-factor loadings, and a two-block correlated-seven covariance violating the single-general-factor tetrad restriction. Use independent EFA/CFA draws and all three ordinal WLSMV fits per CFA sample. Report data-selected factor counts, proper fits and operational/conditional fit-test rejection; do not select a unique winner by the smallest p-value. The higher-order covariance is nested within the correlated-seven family, so both may fit when the higher-order DGP is true. The screening run uses one dataset per structure and 10 permutations only. A prospective full assessment requires 500 permutations, independent replication, category and missingness stress, and a frozen measurement model; no empirical model or sample decision is authorized.

## Retest precision development

The Python driver `retest_calibration.py` uses known joint five-category domain-score distributions, a systematic occasion shift and baseline-dependent follow-up to assess the actual keyed retest analysis. It computes the population agreement functional for both the invited population and the complete-pair distribution. The latter is the coverage target under selective follow-up; coverage of that target does not correct selection bias relative to all invitees. Report interval availability, conditional and unconditional coverage, Wilson intervals, bias, mean width and retained pair counts. Candidate invitation counts are 30, 60, 100 and 160; these are not approved retest sample sizes. The default 20 replicates and 199 bootstrap resamples are a development screen only. Before choosing N, agree acceptable width and retention assumptions, run at least 1000 independent replicates per core cell (2000 near boundaries) with 2000 bootstrap draws, and extend to nonidentical ordinal items, item missingness and plausible empirical distributions. The provisional 100 linked pairs remain a planning target; do not confuse invitations with completed pairs.

## Ordinal-item retest extension

The separate `retest_ordinal_calibration.py` retains the historical score-level screen and adds five heterogeneous ordinal items, conditional independent occasion errors, systematic shift, 20% item MCAR and state-dependent follow-up and item missingness. The selective mechanism depends on the generating latent state, not the observed baseline score. Exact complete-pair score moments are computed by enumerating the six eligible missingness masks and integrating over the 25 latent-state pairs; the target includes the actual 80% scoring rule. The full-data target is distinct from the selected-pair target. Use independent simulations at 60, 100 and 160 invitations; the default run is a smoke screen, not an approved precision analysis. Conditional independence and the finite latent-state distribution are stylised assumptions, not estimated properties of AI-MRFS.
