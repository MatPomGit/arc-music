# P07 calibration v0.3.0

The 44-cell screening grid has 18 core MAR cells (80/160/320 participants, 10/25/40% marginal creation prevalence, latent contrast 0/.4, 30% combined expected loss), 24 stress cells (160 participants, 10/25% prevalence, 0/.2/.6, 15/45% loss, MCAR/MNAR) and two unmeasured-confounding cells. Each participant has 21 potential windows; random propensities, random slopes, AR(1) residuals and bounded rounded outcomes are modeled. Distinct missing baseline, exposure and outcome stages are generated. Under informative missingness the product of marginal stage rates is not the realized complete fraction. Report both.

The production estimator is used directly. Reference coefficients are independently simulated from large complete-data samples per prevalence/latent contrast/confounding combination. Reference estimates have finite uncertainty. Zero rejection is not automatically type-I error when the observed-scale reference is nonzero (especially under confounding); coverage refers to the reference, not the latent treatment parameter. Report failures, mean switcher clusters, valid fraction, bias, coverage and Monte Carlo standard errors. Conditional rejection among successful fits is not unconditional design assurance. No empirical inference or sample-size recommendation follows until results are inspected.

CI executes 3 smoke cells × 20 replications and a separate 44-cell screen × 200 replications. Full retained-cell calibration should use at least 1000 replicates, separately vary autocorrelation, slope heterogeneity, window frequency and dropout, and use pilot-informed creation prevalence. No numerical results are claimed before execution. The grid omits explicitly modeled ongoing creation, missed timing and informative withdrawal beyond stage loss; add these in stress calibration. H2/H3 power is not yet calibrated.
