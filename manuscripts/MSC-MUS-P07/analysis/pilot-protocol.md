# P07: measurement and feasibility pilot v0.7.0

PROSPECTIVE. No participant recruitment or data. Obtain institutional ethics approval before interviews, approve participant information, consent, recruitment channels, data handling and distress response. No paid generation, prescribed creation or emotional induction. No lyrics or prompts collected. Study is not therapy or real-time crisis monitoring.

## Phase A: cognitive interviews
Two successive rounds of 5–8 Polish-speaking adult routine creators; recruit varied usage frequency and experience. Use neutral fictional situations and voluntary think-aloud. Probe distinction between current pleasantness and activation, desired vs actual activation, listening vs active selection, ongoing creation vs initiation within first hour, and first-hour recall despite a delayed response. Check whether anticipation of a prompt changes intention. Document revisions and repeat critical probes. Do not copy standardized instrument items without rights.

## Phase B: feasibility
15–25 different consenting volunteers, 21 days each, three brief assessments per planned day. Freeze schedule and collection version before enrollment. Record planned and active prompts, baseline responses, ongoing exclusions, exposure and outcome availability, withdrawal and burden. Assess per-participant creation prevalence among known eligible exposures and complete switcher fraction, with participant-level bootstrap uncertainty; report uncertainty and selection limitations rather than treating windows as independent. Report observed complete-window counts, timing distributions, floor/ceiling, within-person SD and missingness by day. Record qualitative burden and unexpected reporting difficulty.

Provisional feasibility review triggers: active baseline completion below 70%; first-hour exposure ascertainment below 80% of valid baselines; H1 complete windows below 60% of valid baselines; median participant-level known-exposure prevalence below 10%; fewer than 60% of enrollees provide at least one complete window of each exposure type; more than 50% of a rating at one endpoint; repeated major misunderstanding of timing or construct. These are review triggers, not validated psychometric cutoffs or automatic exclusion criteria. Report both planned and active denominators, including people with zero responses. Investigate missingness and withdrawal before interpreting rates. Do not alter measures to obtain significant pilot effects.

## Decision and freeze
Recalibrate design against plausible lower bounds of creation prevalence, attrition and complete switcher counts. If feasibility is low, consider longer follow-up or more independently scheduled windows with revised burden assessment and calibration. Do not preferentially prompt when creation is anticipated, as this changes the target. Justify smallest meaningful association separately from the pilot effect estimate; pilot n is too small to estimate it precisely. Register final sample target, estimand, timing, missingness procedures, instruments and all changes before confirmatory recruitment. Pilot participants excluded from confirmation.

Withdrawal: cease further prompts and collection immediately at the documented offset-aware cutoff, retain the original schedule for denominator accounting only to the extent allowed by consent and institutional policy. A window already initiated can have partial pre-cutoff responses; a later scheduled window is cancelled, not missed. No data at or after cutoff may enter analysis. A request to erase already collected data is separate and must be handled under the approved procedure before export. Do not infer consent or deletion authority from the cessation log. Only synthetic examples in Git.

Report four mutually exclusive participant categories for each endpoint: no complete known window, noncreation only, creation only, both. Compare with exposure support among valid baselines before outcome loss. Include all enrolled participants, not only responders. Noncreation is heterogeneous; listening, other activities and rest are descriptive alternatives, not a standardized control or post-baseline adjustment. The absence of observed switching need not mean structural nonpositivity. Pilot-based sample feasibility and SESOI remain open.
