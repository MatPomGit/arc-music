# P07 rare-creation and withdrawal calibration v0.5.0

A 24-cell grid (12 per sample size 160/320) with 1000 replicates per cell: creation prevalence 5/10/25%, latent contrast 0/.4, final-day withdrawal 15/45%. The existing 30% expected stage loss is compounded with 15% ongoing-creation exclusion and 10% independent timing failure. Ongoing windows are ineligible by design, not missing exposures. Ongoing status is associated with pre-window intention, and withdrawal depends on the preceding day's underlying affect and intention. These covariates may be unavailable in the actual observed ledger, so withdrawal is informative rather than guaranteed MAR. Final-day attrition is distinct from overall window loss. Serial correlation .4 and random slope SD .25 remain fixed, as in the original generator. Prevalence is specified before selection; realized observed prevalence is reported.

Separate large complete-data references (20,000 simulated participants per prevalence and latent contrast per sample-size job) include the ongoing exclusion but no missingness, timing failure or withdrawal. The reference has finite simulation error and describes eligible switchers under that generator, not a causal effect. Rejection conditional on successful estimation and unconditional rejection among all planned replications are both reported. Fifty switchers is a descriptive design diagnostic, not a validated adequacy threshold. Lower fifth percentile switcher count and coverage/rejection Monte Carlo errors are recorded. The selected complete switcher population may change under missingness.

Execution: python stress.py --n 160 --reps 1000 --reference-n 20000 and repeat for 320. CI emits exact-head JSON and environment metadata in separate artifacts. No result or recruitment target is claimed before the jobs finish. The grid does not yet vary serial correlation independently, model MNAR outcome selection in this extension, or establish H2/H3 power. Those require subsequent calibration and pilot-informed choices. No participant data.
