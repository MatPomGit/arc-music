# P09: prospective measurement and fidelity protocol v0.1.0

Research proposal; no expert ratings, interviews, pilot or ethics approval are claimed. Candidate gates below require team approval.

## Construct map

Target: perceived understanding of the relation (including absence of a relation) between one preselected autobiographical event and current self at T0, T1 and T2. Item 1: self-description; item 2: values; item 3: life-story placement. Three complete 0–10 integer ratings averaged without weights. Distance is a distinct single rating. Not objective coherence, centrality, positive appraisal, memory accuracy or trait identity. Do not interpret low endorsement of an event's importance as low understanding.

## Expert review and cognitive interviews

Recruit 6–8 independent reviewers covering autobiographical memory, music psychology, measurement and Polish-language survey design. Each independently rates item relevance and clarity from 1 to 4, comments on omissions, redundancy, recall frame, endpoints and potential pressure toward positive interpretation. Calculate proportion of 3/4 ratings separately for relevance and clarity; report numerator/denominator and comments. A provisional revision flag is a proportion below .80 or any substantive unresolved construct concern; not a validity certificate. Evaluate comprehensiveness at scale level. Record changes and rationale, not personal data.

Two rounds of 5–8 eligible adults with varied musical experience and AI familiarity, subject to approval. Use think-aloud/paraphrase and neutral fictional vignettes for probing, not as substitutes for experimental autobiographical events. Probes: what does understanding mean; can a trivial or identity-irrelevant event be understood well; do you answer about importance, liking, emotion or factual certainty; what distinguishes 0, 5 and 10; does the same wording refer to the original event after seven days? Collect coded difficulties only, no event text. Any critical misinterpretation triggers revision and rechecking; no fixed count proves saturation. Freeze wording, response anchors, timing and scoring after resolution.

## Pilot and feasibility

Separate N=30–50, balanced assignment where feasible, excluded from confirmation. Report recruitment flow, technical success, item-level missingness, completion by arm, T2 retention, distribution, floor/ceiling, inter-item associations and uncertainty. Provisional review flags: more than 20% at either composite boundary; more than 10% missing at any item at a valid visit; less than 80% timely original-event T2 assessment; arm difference in retention greater than 10 percentage points. These are engineering/planning flags, not validated psychometric cutoffs. Report exact binomial intervals for rates; small sample means flags are uncertain. Investigate rather than optimise based on observed treatment effects.

Check expected associations with perceived representational fit and reflective elaboration; distinguish liking, mood, event importance and centrality. Associations are descriptive, without rigid magnitude thresholds or causal mediation. Prefer item profiles and covariance estimates to a forced reflective model. A just-identified three-item CFA cannot establish model fit. No definitive latent invariance or reliability claim; no alpha/omega for single-item distance. Independent larger validation would be needed if latent interpretation is intended.

## Manipulation and assessment order

At T0: original event chosen privately; baseline endpoints; importance; modality preferences; prior AI experience and expectation. After allocation: accurate origin disclosure, preview and selection logs, active exposure duration and technical interruptions. At T1: confirm event and collect endpoints before process questions. Candidate process ratings (0–10, descriptive only): fit to event, ease of reflection, perceived contribution, ownership, liking, novelty, effort, current affect and ability to distinguish selected artefact provenance. Provenance comprehension uses categorical choices including unsure, not a numerical score. At T2: confirm original event and assess endpoints before asking about later engagement. No mandatory replay. Log no text, personal prompts or narratives.

Predefine acceptable task completion and exposure-equivalence rules after stimulus pilot, before confirmation; report failures by arm, not exclusion. Do not require statistical non-significance to demonstrate equivalence. If exposure or preference differs, narrow interpretation to assignment package. Invalid assessment of a defined event remains missing. Do not classify a negative self-event connection as undefined.

## Approval gate

Review instrument version, coded interview findings, expert rationale, rights of comparators, data minimisation, accessibility, pilot flow, follow-up feasibility, SESOI and sample size before confirmation. No participant-facing production deployment or recruitment before separate approval.
