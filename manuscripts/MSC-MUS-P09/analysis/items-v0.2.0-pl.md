# P09 candidate item bank v0.2.0 (not frozen)

Original event-specific perceived understanding, not objective coherence, memory accuracy or global narrative identity. Retain items-pl.md as historical wording. Instruction: „Odpowiadaj o tym samym, wybranym wcześniej zdarzeniu. Możesz dobrze rozumieć zdarzenie również wtedy, gdy nie widzisz jego związku z tym, kim jesteś. Nie musisz przypisywać mu pozytywnego znaczenia.” Response scale 0 wcale to 10 w bardzo dużym stopniu; skip allowed.

| Field at T0/T1/T2 | Proposed single-focus wording | Content |
|---|---|---|
| understanding j1 | Na ile jasne jest dla Ciebie, jak to zdarzenie odnosi się do tego, kim jesteś? | Perceived self-event relation (including no relation) |
| understanding j2 | Na ile rozumiesz związek tego zdarzenia z tym, co jest dla Ciebie ważne? | Perceived values relation |
| understanding j3 | Na ile rozumiesz miejsce tego zdarzenia w swojej dotychczasowej historii życia? | Perceived life-story placement |
| distance j (T0/T1) | Na ile w tej chwili postrzegasz to zdarzenie z szerszej perspektywy? | Current perceived perspective, not general capacity |

Three complete ratings form a candidate content composite; no latent homogeneity assumed. Current perspective does not establish self-distancing mechanism or benefit. Probe 0/5/10, event with no identity relevance, difference between current perspective and ability to take perspective, mood, memory certainty, and importance. Do not retain event text. Same wording across visits; check response shift and longitudinal comparability. Separate ratings of fit, liking, effort, expectancy, importance, current affect, perceived elaboration; no postassignment adjustment.

Pilot: expert review and two iterative cognitive interview rounds, then separate 30–50 participants only after approval. Use standardized neutral vignettes for comprehension (not replacement for autobiographical outcomes), examine item distributions and convergent associations with perceived elaboration and fit, discriminant associations with mood/liking/centrality, and responsiveness to a standardized neutral perspective exercise in an independent pilot component. Do not select by arm effect. Establish practical equivalence margins for duration and access before pilot; compare intervals, not nonsignificant tests. The music/visual packages differ in modality, temporal properties and possibly provenance; the experiment cannot isolate AI or identify mediation. No change to historical score simulations or final instrument freeze.
