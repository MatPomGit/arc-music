# AIM-ER proposition-to-protocol map, v1.0.0

Scientific owner: Mateusz Pomianek. This matrix compares planned tests with the original P01 predictions, not completed empirical findings. EXACT means an operationally matching dedicated protocol, not an approved or validated study; PARTIAL means a related planned endpoint, manipulation or exploratory analysis but not an exact test; UNTESTED means no dedicated matching protocol in P02–P09. P01 contains the conceptual designs for all propositions, but does not count as an executed or dedicated companion study.

| Prediction | Construct and required measure | Population, design and timing | Target estimand | Companion study and status | Remaining exact test |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| CP | Common 0–10 goal attainment, comparable across articulation and reinterpretation; separate state clarity and reinterpretation | Eligible adults, independently randomized goal × affordance 2 × 2, baseline and immediate endpoint | Intersection-union of positive matched-minus-mismatched simple effects; mean contrast secondary summary | UNTESTED; P01 conceptual design only, P04/P08 manipulate different affordances | Dedicated factorial trial with comparable scale, fidelity, outcome-independent ascertainment and calibrated missingness |
| H1 | Immediate distress reduction in a relief-goal session, same pre/post distress scale | Adults pursuing relief, random assignment to AI creation vs duration-matched neutral activity | Baseline-adjusted assigned-group contrast in immediate distress | PARTIAL [P07](../../MSC-MUS-P07/main.tex): observed creation-window valence, not randomized relief intervention | Dedicated randomized relief-goal trial with distress rather than general valence |
| H2 | Personal emotional resonance after common expressive writing; distress separate | Adults after common writing, randomized generated musical rendering vs reading identical text; immediate | Assigned-group contrast in resonance | PARTIAL [P09](../../MSC-MUS-P09/main.tex): music vs visual representation, delayed understanding; [P03](../../MSC-MUS-P03/main.tex): recalled resonance associations | Matched writing/rendering comparison with common resonance rating |
| H3 | Attributed authorial contribution and distinct ownership rating; objective control measured or held comparable | Adult creators, contribution measured before ownership; prospective association within comparable objective control | Adjusted contribution–ownership association | PARTIAL [P08](../../MSC-MUS-P08/main.tex): authority on ownership is randomized, attributed contribution exploratory | Prospectively specified adjusted association using non-overlapping measures and comparable control |
| H4 | State agency and actual opportunity for meaningful editing | Adult creators, randomized editable vs selection-only interface, common generator/time; immediate pre-sharing | Assigned-group agency contrast | PARTIAL [P08](../../MSC-MUS-P08/main.tex): decision authority/execution factorial, agency secondary; not editable vs selection-only | Exact interface contrast, independently defined outcome and valid missingness strategy |
| H5 | Trait difficulty identifying emotions (authorized DIF-specific measure), baseline/post unaided emotional clarity | Adults performing musical task; trait before assignment, clarity before and after | Adjusted trait DIF–post clarity slope | PARTIAL [P04](../../MSC-MUS-P04/main.tex): PAQ-S total general alexithymia, not DIF; H2 clarity association | Authorized valid DIF-specific instrument, separate moderation test for label prompts |
| H6 | State rumination after relief, immediate and delayed distress/relief | Adult creators, repeated natural sessions, baseline, post-session, fixed delayed follow-up | Within-person rumination coefficient on delayed relief conditional on baseline distress and immediate relief | PARTIAL [P06](../../MSC-MUS-P06/main.tex): brooding and next-day interference; [P07](../../MSC-MUS-P07/main.tex): later valence | Delayed distress endpoint and ordered state rumination; no substitution of interference or valence |
| H7 | Perceived social validation and subsequent goal attainment | Adults opting into standardized feedback about non-sensitive artefact; randomized supportive vs neutral feedback | Assigned-group contrasts in validation and subsequent attainment | UNTESTED; [P02](../../MSC-MUS-P02/main.tex) social accounts qualitative, not effect test | Dedicated feedback experiment with ethical consent and comparable messages |
| H8 | Continuous baseline musical expertise, state agency, reduced creative control | Adult creators, randomized interface, expertise preassignment; agency after task | Expertise × interface assignment interaction | PARTIAL [P08](../../MSC-MUS-P08/main.tex): expertise moderation exploratory, authority/execution rather than original control contrast | Prespecified continuous interaction, calibrated power, not exploratory confirmation |
| H9 | Baseline regulatory flexibility, unsuccessful session and opportunity, repeat/switch/stop transitions | Adult creators, prospective decisions following unsuccessful sessions | Conditional association of flexibility with transition probabilities | PARTIAL [P06](../../MSC-MUS-P06/main.tex): flexibility exploratory; [P02](../../MSC-MUS-P02/main.tex) narrative alternatives | Prospective threshold, eligibility for each transition and validated baseline measure |
| H10 | Immediate enjoyment at first and last standardized sessions, novelty separate | Adults randomized to fixed-version AI vs stable comparator, repeated sessions and independent final offer | Between-arm difference in mean first-to-last enjoyment changes | UNTESTED; [P07](../../MSC-MUS-P07/main.tex) natural creation windows and valence are not enjoyment by randomized tool across repeated standardized tasks | Dedicated longitudinal randomized comparison; first/final missingness and novelty mediation separate |

P02 is qualitative discovery, P05 is psychometric development, and P03 is a cross-sectional association study. None is a substitute for an exact experiment. P04, P08 and P09 concern distinct assigned intervention packages. P06 and P07 concern different observational targets. Any shared cohort or repeated analysis requires a common dataset identifier, explicit disclosure, distinct primary questions and assessment of redundancy. No planned companion study is an exact full test of a P01 prediction as currently specified; partial overlap may still be informative. No claim of empirical validation, independent replication, causal mediation or authorization follows.
