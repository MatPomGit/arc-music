
# Neuropsychological transfer matrix
Each proposed AIM-ER pathway below remains a testable hypothesis; "direct" means direct evidence for the source task, not for the transfer to AIM-ER.

| Pathway | Source-task evidence | Transfer limitation | Discriminating AIM-ER test |
|---|---|---|---|
| Auditory appraisal | Koelsch 2014, review of musical emotion (indirect) | Perceived emotion differs from induced affect and intentional regulation | Manipulate semantic fidelity on matched audio; independent expression ratings, experienced affect and goal attainment |
| Reward/selection | Salimpoor 2011 PET/fMRI listening; Ferreri 2019 pharmacological listening (direct for listening) | Reward does not establish beneficial regulation; no dopamine inference from EDA/HRV/EEG | Separate anticipation, liking, repeated generation and goal attainment; yoked exposure |
| Autobiographical retrieval | Janata 2009 familiar songs (indirect) | Newly generated cues have no established prior song–memory association | Cross initial autobiographical relevance with later familiarity; blinded fidelity ratings |
| Reappraisal and distance | Ochsner 2012, Kross 2011 non-musical tasks (indirect) | Distance may reflect disengagement, not helpful reinterpretation | Randomise perspective transformation with constant audio and decision budget; assess interpretation then later functioning |
| Affect labelling | Lieberman 2007 emotion labelling task (indirect) | Prompting adds generation, editing and listening | Compare label versus neutral semantic operation with equivalent exposure |
| Agency and ownership | He 2026 automation; Zhang 2026 abstract; Swarbrick & Saarikallio 2026 exploratory (direct co-creation evidence) | No neural marker or identified regulation mediator; workflow bundles | Randomise editability, assess state agency and distinct ownership before outcome; no mediation inference from correlation |
| Perseveration | Carlson 2015 music-use associations; Nolen-Hoeksema 2008 (indirect) | Negative affect or repeated listening need not be maladaptive | Temporally order rumination and follow-up functioning; compare reflection and brooding; sensitivity to dropout |
| Overall efficacy | Peters 2024 heterogeneous musicking; Jin 2026 low-certainty AI/personalisation evidence (indirect) | No transfer of pooled effect to AI creation | Independently randomise goal and affordance, include yoked and non-AI comparator |
