# H3/H5 measurement specification (prospective, not validated)

H3: before creative task, record prior musical/AI experience, mood and assigned editing options; log available and exercised operations separately. Assess attribution of perceived human contribution after final choice, before ownership; then assess felt belonging using non-overlapping candidate items and record effort. Counterbalance order in a separate validation sample to assess order effects; compare reverse-order associations and report adjusted estimates with intervals. Ownership is not legal authorship. Confirm discriminant validity with a prespecified correlated-factor model versus a one-factor model, report latent correlations with intervals, and do not claim independent constructs if the interval is consistent with unity. If insufficient validated measures, treat as exploratory.

H5: prefer the general difficulty-identifying-feelings score of the existing Polish PAQ; TAS-20-POL-R DIF is an alternative if justified before recruitment. Record the exact form, scoring instructions and applicable administration rights in the approved protocol, and do not substitute a total alexithymia score or switch instruments after inspecting outcomes. Use baseline state clarity on a distinct measure and post-task state clarity with common time anchors. Record baseline depressive symptoms and negative affect separately. An additional unaided articulation task uses independent blinded raters; score specificity and differentiation according to a piloted rubric, report interrater ICC and intervals. Model post-task clarity on baseline clarity and continuous trait DIF; adjust prespecified pre-task covariates; no causal claim. An interaction with prompting support is separate. For a multi-item state measure, assess longitudinal measurement invariance before interpreting change; for a single rating, evaluate response-process comparability and distributional sensitivity instead; avoid duplicating trait items in state assessment. Neither a new scale nor an empirical study is required to publish P01.
