## Manipulation and measurement

The same audio previews, instructions about the creative goal, preference selection and opportunity to stop are presented in every cell. Both preview controls stay available during a fixed interval. After preference is locked, show the selected option and who selected it. Reserve a further ten seconds in both execution conditions. In the manual condition one confirmation queues application at the common deadline. An expired manual confirmation leaves the previous version available for listening. In the automatic condition application is queued by the interface. The action never changes the selected option. Use native buttons and keyboard access. Provide text for deadlines and playback status. Confirm whether the referenced version was heard separately from playback telemetry.

Generate one manifest per bank version with duration, sample rate, loudness, clipping, source model/version, generation date, permission basis and file hashes. Four binary revisions give sixteen final combinations. Test paths offline with `python fixture_audio.py /tmp/p08-fixture`. The procedural fixtures lack provenance as AI-produced stimuli and are for technical verification only. Before pilot, prepare an authorised generated bank matched for integrated loudness, length, timbral salience and two-option distinguishability. Freeze the common starter and preview paths. Avoid personal prompts and third-party samples. Do not assume that service access provides redistribution rights.

The server must bind assignment and material version to the eligible consented pseudonym. Store allocation outside Git. The transactional prototype already serialises assignments within strata. With a separately concealed random sequence order, consume one of sixteen balanced system schedules per eligible allocation, stratified by assigned execution within system authority. Return only the current assignment and schedule. Keep consent, withdrawal and contact mappings outside the analytical data. Do not log raw browser credentials or identifiable audio. Playback and response events use pseudonymous identifiers and versioned IDs. The server retains failed steps and the reason for failure. An endpoint for withdrawal requires deletion or restricted retention under an approved policy. Nothing in this prototype contacts participants.

## Pilot and decision rules

Perform 6 to 10 cognitive interviews balanced over novice and trained backgrounds. Ask participants to paraphrase the distinction between selecting and applying a revision, describe what each ownership and agency item refers to, and identify whose work the item makes them consider. Collect expert ratings of relevance, clarity, domain membership and likely construct contamination. Resolve disagreement before freezing items. Include examples of ownership without manual operation and operation without final decision authority. Keep effort, liking, autonomy and attributed authorship as distinct explanatory probes. Administer manipulation checks after outcome ratings to avoid priming.

After authorisation, run a 30 to 50 person feasibility pilot. Review by assigned cell: correct identification of both manipulation roles, proportion of heard versions, complete ratings conditional on hearing, previews available, actual preview exposure and task time. Provisional design gates are at least 80% joint role comprehension overall, at least 85% heard versions overall, no cell below 70% on either metric, and at least 90% successful playback among users who proceed to the listening step. Report binomial intervals and inspect discrepancy by device, keyboard use and training. These gates guide redesign. They do not establish statistical equivalence. Freeze meaningful timing and audibility tolerances before confirmation and check robustness to modest alterations of their boundaries. No effect observed in the feasibility pilot determines the target effect.

Choose the randomised sample after agreeing on the smallest meaningful change on the 0 to 10 scale, estimating outcome dispersion and availability in the feasibility pilot, and rerunning the planning grid with those ranges. Provision for H1 separately from H2 and H3, which form a Holm family. If 90% primary power under approved assumptions exceeds feasible recruitment, defer confirmation. Report structural absence separately from missing ratings. This document does not launch a study.

For each assigned cell distinguish confirmed hearing of an assessable version, confirmed non-hearing and unresolved exposure. Missing item ratings after confirmed hearing are not structural absence. If any hearing is false or unknown, full-cohort numerical sensitivity and confirmatory tests are withheld; heard-only contrasts remain descriptive. Review hearing feasibility before confirmation; do not select a postassignment survivor estimand after seeing results.

See measurement-validation.md for proposed expert and cognitive interview procedures and timing equivalence rules. The two timing margins must be approved before inspecting pilot differences. Record joint role comprehension and separate playback initiation, success and hearing; preserve unknown flags.

## Versioned allocation candidate

The new assignment_ledger.py is the offline authoritative candidate for empirical exports; allocation.py remains historical synthetic-only. It binds the bank SHA-256, schema, arm, stratum, UTC timestamp and concealed four-bit system schedule, consumed atomically with allocation. All four cells receive separate balanced schedule cycles, including participant-authority cells where the system schedule is a nonbinding comparator. Return only the current schedule and assignment, never future slots. The authenticated server must verify gates and bank rights; boolean parameters alone do not authenticate. Reconcile all allocated rows before analysis, even missing outcomes. Any withdrawal blocks export pending approved retention policy; do not recycle assignments. No deployment or permission to collect data.

The candidate v0.2.0 item bank separates experienced action-outcome contingency from factual role checks and felt ownership from attribution. Preserve original items as historical; no score freeze or hierarchy change without approval. Use the same nonbinding system schedule in participant-authority cells for comparator/fidelity accounting, not to override choice.
