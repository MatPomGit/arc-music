# P08 targeted source audit

Scope: decision authority, execution, automation, ownership and agency during AI-assisted creation. Searches used titles and concept combinations in publisher indexes, PubMed and the NeurIPS proceedings on 27 November 2026. This is a targeted update. It cannot establish exhaustive priority.

| Source | Verified location | Supported claim and transfer boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Pierce et al. (2001), DOI 10.5465/amr.2001.4378028 | DOI and publication metadata; routes triangulated in the ownership literature | Control, knowing the object and investing the self are theoretical antecedents. Organisational theory alone does not validate the P08 measures or predict an effect size. |
| Pierce et al. (2003), DOI 10.1037/1089-2680.7.1.84 | Publication metadata | Broad integrative review of psychological ownership. Direct PDF claim-location confirmation remains open. |
| Draxler et al. (2024), DOI 10.1145/3637875 | Author preprint and abstract, arXiv:2303.03283 | In personalised text generation, ownership and public authorship declarations diverged. Faster or different music production conditions may not transfer. |
| He (2026), DOI 10.3389/fpsyg.2026.1835406 | Full text §§3.2–3.5 and Table 2 | Manipulation combines different levels of task involvement, automation and creative activity. Reported associations across ownership, agency and load do not identify independent effects of decision authority and execution; no transfer of effect size. |
| Haggard (2017), DOI 10.1038/nrn.2017.14 | Publisher Key Points and abstract | Explicit agency judgements can be theoretically distinguished from implicit action-outcome measures. No inference about neural correlates of P08 from its questionnaire. |
| Parasuraman et al. (2000), DOI 10.1109/3468.844354 | PubMed abstract and publication metadata | Selection and action implementation are distinct stages of automation. A taxonomy does not establish empirical factor independence. |
| Deci and Ryan (2000), DOI 10.1207/S15327965PLI1104_01 | Publisher abstract and publication metadata | Autonomy concerns the quality of motivation and self-endorsement. More available control need not guarantee autonomy. |
| Krol et al. (2025), arXiv:2509.25834 | NeurIPS proceedings abstract | The ecological study links reliance on musicians' initial inputs to reported ownership. It does not estimate a factorial treatment contrast or establish effects in novices. |
